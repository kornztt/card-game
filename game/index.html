How to play

  • Click a card, then click the enemy to strike — or click a Guard card to shield yourself.
  • Each card costs energy. Energy refills every turn and grows by 1 (up to 6).
  • Flame > Leaf > Stone > Tide > Flame: an advantaged strike deals +2 damage.
